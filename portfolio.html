FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you travel for destination weddings outside Chhattisgarh?

Absolutely. Praveen Studio has covered weddings and pre-wedding sessions across India and destination locations. Travel and accommodation costs for outstation work are charged separately. Reach out via the contact page to discuss your requirements.

How far in advance should I book my wedding photography?

We recommend booking at least 3–6 months before your wedding date to secure your slot, especially for peak wedding season (October–February). For popular destinations and dates, early booking is essential as we accept limited weddings per month to maintain the quality of our work.

How many photographs will we receive after the wedding?

The number of delivered images depends on your package and the duration of coverage. Typically, a full-day wedding coverage delivers 400–800 professionally edited photographs. You will receive high-resolution images via a private online gallery. For exact numbers, discuss your package during the consultation.

What is the typical delivery timeline for wedding photos?

We deliver a set of same-day highlight images within a week of the wedding. The full edited gallery is delivered within 4–6 weeks. Wedding films and cinematic edits may take 6–10 weeks depending on complexity. We keep you updated throughout the process.

Do you offer pre-wedding photography sessions separately?

Yes! Pre-wedding photography can be booked independently of wedding coverage. We help you choose the perfect location — from golden fields around Raipur to scenic outdoor spots across Chhattisgarh or Goa. Sessions are typically 2–4 hours and deliver 50–150 edited images.
