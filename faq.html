Questions & Answers

Everything you need to know about working with Praveen Studio.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

We Believe in
Clear Answers

Do you cover destination weddings?

Yes. We cover celebrations across India and selected international destinations. Our team has experience with destination weddings in Goa, Rajasthan, Kerala, and beyond. Please share your destination details via our Wedding Details Questionnaire and we will advise on the best approach for your specific celebration.

How is the coverage team decided?

Recommended according to your functions, venue, guest scale and requirements. Every wedding is different, and we recommend a team that is specifically suited to your celebration — not a fixed package. After understanding your needs, we propose the right team composition for your day.

Do you help with pre-wedding planning?

Yes — our team supports timelines, coordination and pre-wedding planning. We help with location selection, outfit guidance, music direction, travel coordination and timeline planning. We believe the planning process is as important as the shoot itself, and we guide every couple through it with patience and care.

How are our memories protected?

Every project follows a structured backup, data-management and delivery workflow. All images and footage are backed up on multiple independent storage systems from the moment they are captured — and maintained securely throughout the editing and delivery process. Your memories are never at risk.

How do we confirm our booking?

After our consultation and once a suitable plan is agreed upon, your date is secured through booking formalities and an initial payment. We recommend enquiring as early as possible, as our availability fills up — particularly for prime wedding seasons and destination dates.

How early should we book Praveen Studio?

We recommend enquiring at least 6–12 months in advance for prime wedding dates, and as early as possible for destination and multi-day weddings. Popular dates — especially during wedding seasons and holiday periods — book out well in advance. Share your date via our questionnaire to check availability.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery timelines depend on the scope of your project — the number of functions, films, and album requirements. We provide clear delivery timelines at the time of booking and maintain regular updates throughout the process. We never compromise on quality for the sake of speed.

Do you do Goa pre-wedding shoots?

Yes. Goa is one of our most requested pre-wedding destinations. We support every aspect of planning your Goa pre-wedding — from location selection and travel coordination to outfit guidance and timing. Visit our Journal for our complete Goa Pre-Wedding Photography Guide.

Do you have fixed packages or pricing on the website?

No. We do not publish fixed pricing because every wedding is different. The team, coverage, experiences and deliverables are all personalised to your specific celebration. After understanding your requirements, we prepare a personalised proposal. Please share your details via the Wedding Details Questionnaire to begin.

What is the Same Day Edit Experience?

The Same Day Edit is one of our most requested signature experiences. While your wedding celebration is still ongoing, our dedicated editing team works in real time to select, edit and present your wedding highlights on an LED screen — on the same day. It is one of the most emotional moments of any wedding. Learn more on our Experiences page.
